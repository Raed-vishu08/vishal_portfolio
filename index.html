Who I Am

Building Engineers,
Not Just Robots.

I'm a Robotics Instructor and Embedded Systems Engineer who believes the best technical education happens when students build, break, and fix real things. I've taught 1,000+ engineering students across live workshops, structured courses, and hands-on lab sessions — from complete beginners to final-year engineers.

My hands-on experience spans the full robotics stack: working with the Ainex Humanoid and Puppy Pi, delivering workshops at institutions like NIAT Delhi, and teaching ROS, IoT, and embedded systems at NxtWave Disruptive Technologies.

"Theory serves the build, and the build reveals the gaps in theory — that's how I design every session."

My academic background includes a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication (CGPA: 8.76) from Central University of Karnataka and a research internship at NIT Surathkal under an ISRO-sponsored RESPOND project. I was also a Grand Finalist at Smart India Hackathon (Ministry of Power) and served as IEEE SB-CUK SIGHT Chair.
